## Purpose

Transform note-like writing into a story. Add context, transitions, and emotional connection while keeping the author's direct, understated voice.

## What to Improve

### Opening

- Establish context before diving into technical details
- Show the situation or problem that led to this exploration
- Give the reader a reason to care

### Transitions Between Sections

Add connecting tissue between sections. Examples:
- "Once I accepted that X wasn't working, I looked at alternatives."
- "I'd already learned this the hard way."
- "What if I could get the benefits of both approaches?"

### Decision Points

Show the thinking process:
- Why did you choose this path?
- What were you weighing?
- What made you change direction?

### Endings

- Connect back to the opening
- Reflect on what you learned
- Leave the reader with something to think about

## What NOT to Do

- Don't add unnecessary words or filler
- Don't make it overly emotional or dramatic
- Don't change the technical content
- Don't add enthusiasm or hype ("Amazing!", "Super easy!")
- Keep the author's direct, understated voice

## Author's Voice

- Direct and concise
- First-person narrative
- Understated, avoids hyperbole
- Shows the journey, including dead ends
- Questions design intent

## Output Format

### Changes Made

List each change:
- **Location**: Where in the post
- **Before**: Original text (brief quote)
- **After**: New text
- **Why**: Brief explanation

### Revised Version

Provide the complete revised post (excluding frontmatter).
