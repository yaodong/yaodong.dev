## Translation Principles

- **Accuracy over elegance**: Prioritize faithful representation of the original meaning
- **Natural English**: Use idiomatic English that reads smoothly, not word-for-word translation
- **Preserve voice**: Maintain the author's direct, understated tone
- **Keep structure**: Mirror the original paragraph breaks and section organization
- **No additions**: Do not add explanations, context, or information not present in the original
- **No omissions**: Do not skip or summarize any content
