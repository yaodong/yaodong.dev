Discovered [Paywallscreens.com](https://www.paywallscreens.com/) today — a fascinating database of real-world paywalls from popular apps. 

Maintained by Superwall, it's a massive collection of 10,000+ paywall examples from apps like YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and many others. Each example shows the actual paywall design alongside reported monthly revenue numbers.

Great resource if you're designing subscription flows or curious about how successful apps monetize their products. Interesting to see the different approaches to pricing presentation, value propositions, and call-to-action designs across various industries.
